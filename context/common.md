## 2. Target Audience
Two groups, both of whom hire, buy, or vouch for engineering talent:

1. **Technical founders, CTOs, and engineering/AI leads** at small-to-mid companies. They hire and they buy.
2. **Senior and staff engineers.** They refer people and judge who is credible.

Both groups have watched AI demos work in a sandbox and then fail in production. Both are tired of generic AI hype. What they respond to: proof that something ran in production, how it was measured, and what broke along the way. What they're wary of: wasted budget, silent failures, and vendors relabeling old scripts as "agents." They trust specifics and named sources over claims, and they disengage from anything that reads like a pitch.

## 3. Strategic Objectives
- Build a following of the right people, not raw reach. No engagement bait to hit a follower number.
- Generate inbound DMs and profile views from founders, senior engineers, and tech leaders, tracked weekly.
- Earn connection first. Jobs and paying clients come later, out of the relationships, not from pitching.
- Build credibility through specific, verifiable, production-tested experience, never broad or unverifiable claims.

**90-day target:** 10,000 followers, weighted toward the two audience groups above, not raw reach. Progress is measured by inbound DMs and profile views per week, not likes.
